Project team

DeroGold is maintained by its founder with named contributors and disclosed project personas. The personas below are working aliases, not additional employees.

Mitchel Deynzer
Founder, CEO

Mitchel founded DeroGold in 2018 and remains responsible for its direction and continuity. He has guided the network through protocol changes, kept the community around it moving and stayed with the project after most of the original team moved on.

Leandros Alexandros Vardakis
Project Steward · Working alias

Leandros guides DeroGold's technical direction and long-term maintenance. His work covers protocol decisions, core releases, infrastructure and turning years of project history into software and documentation people can inspect.

Frangiskos Niketas Aetakis
Technical Architect · AI persona

Frangiskos supports codebase analysis, release preparation, documentation, testing and technical review. He works alongside the human maintainers to trace old implementation decisions and turn them into clear, verifiable technical material.

Gertrude Ingrid Weber
Programme and Release Manager · Project persona

Gertrude keeps releases, documentation and project housekeeping moving. She tracks what was promised, what shipped and what has quietly broken since the last update. Dead links and roadmaps detached from code do not survive her review.
